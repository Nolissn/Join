Privacy Policy

Responsible party

Join is a student project developed by Luis Simon, Steven Hoffman, Samuel Schonmeier and Sebastian Opileka as part of a web development bootcamp at the Developer Akademie GmbH. The contact details of the responsible team can be found in the Legal Notice. For any questions about your data, please write to team@join-app.de.

Which data we collect

We only collect the data that you enter yourself while using Join:

  • Account data: your name, your email address and your password when you sign up.
  • Contacts: the name, email address and phone number of the contacts you add.
  • Tasks: the title, description, due date, priority, category, subtasks and assigned contacts of the tasks you create.

If you use the guest log in, you do not need to enter any personal data.

How we use your data

Your data is used only to provide the features of Join: logging you in, showing your board, your contacts and your summary, and greeting you by name. We do not use your data for advertising, we do not create user profiles and we do not sell or pass on your data to third parties, except to the hosting provider named below.

Where your data is stored

Your account data, contacts and tasks are stored in Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore), a database service provided by Google. Google may process this data on servers outside the European Union. You can find more information in the privacy policy of Google: https://policies.google.com/privacy.

Data stored in your browser

While you are logged in, Join uses the session storage of your browser to remember that you are logged in and to temporarily keep your tasks and contacts. This data is removed when you log out or close the browser tab. Firebase may additionally store a technical identifier in your browser to keep the connection to the database. Join does not use tracking cookies or analytics tools.

How we protect your data

  • The board, the contacts and all other personal pages are only available after logging in. Visitors who are not logged in are redirected to the log in page.
  • You are logged out automatically after 30 minutes of inactivity.
  • All data is transmitted to Firebase over an encrypted connection (HTTPS).

As Join is an educational project, we cannot offer the level of security of a commercial product. Please do not enter sensitive information and do not use a password that you also use for other services.

Your rights

You have the right to receive information about the data stored about you and to have it corrected or deleted. You can edit or delete your contacts and tasks yourself at any time in Join. To have your account deleted or for any other request, please contact us at team@join-app.de.

Changes to this privacy policy

We may update this privacy policy when Join changes. The current version is always available on this page.
